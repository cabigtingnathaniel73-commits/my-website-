Activity: The Adobo Recipe

Cultural Background & Fun Facts:

Adobo is one of the most popular and well-known dishes in the Philippines. It is commonly prepared by cooking chicken in a mixture of soy sauce, vinegar, garlic, bay leaves, and black pepper. The word adobo refers to a cooking method that involves marinating and simmering meat in a flavorful sauce. Different regions and families have their own versions of adobo, making the dish unique in many Filipino homes. Chicken adobo is especially popular because it is affordable, easy to prepare, and pairs well with steamed rice. It can also be prepared in advance because its flavor can become richer after resting. This dish is an important part of Filipino food culture and is often enjoyed during family meals, celebrations, and everyday lunches.

Categorized Ingredients

  • Main Proteins
    • Chicken
      • 1 kilogram chicken pieces- cleaned and cut
  • Aromatics & Spices
    • Garlic
      • 6 cloves garlic- crushed or minced
    • Bay Leaves
      • 3 pieces dried bay leaves
    • Black Pepper
      • 1 teaspoon ground black pepper
  • Sauce base
    • Soy Sauce
      • 1/2 cup- any regular brand
    • Vinegar
      • 1/2 cup- preferably cane vinegar
    • Water
      • 1 cup- adjust according to desired sauce
  • Additional Ingredients
    • Cooking Oil
      • 2 tablespoon Cooking oil
    • Sugar
      • 1 teaspoon- optional for a slightly sweet taste

Cooking Process

  1. Prepare the Chicken
    1. Clean and prepare the chicken pieces.
      1. Pat the chicken dry before cooking to help it brown properly
    2. Prepare the garlic and other ingredients.
  2. Mix the Aromatics
    1. Heat the cooking oil in a large pan.
      1. cook the garlic until fragrant, but avoid burning it.
    2. Add the chicken pieces and lightly brown them.
  3. Add the Sauce Ingredients
    1. Pour in the soy sauce and vinegar.
      1. Let the vinegar simmer without stirring for about 2 minutes.
    2. Add the water, bay leaves, and black pepper.
  4. Simmer the Chicken
    1. Cover the pan and simmer over medium-low heat
      1. Cook for approximately 30 to 40 minutes or until the chicken is fully
    2. Turn the chicken occasionally so it absorbs the sauce evenly
  5. Adjust the Flavor
    1. Taste the sauce
      1. Add a small amount of sugar if you prefer a slightly sweeter flavor
    2. Continue simmering until the sauce reaches your preferred thickness
  6. Serve the Chicken Adobo
    1. Turn off the heat and transfer the chicken to a serving plate
      1. Serve hot with freshly cooked steamed rice
    2. Enjoy your homemade chicken adobo with family
